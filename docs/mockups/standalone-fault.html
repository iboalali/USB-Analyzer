Findings for this port

Cable is rated for 3 A, charger offers 5 A measured

The cable's e-marker reports a 3 A current rating. At the negotiated 20 V that caps the contract at 60 W, 40 W below what the charger can deliver.

→ Try a 5 A e-marked cable and watch this figure

Contract settled below the offer measured

60 W agreed against a 100 W offer. Explained entirely by the cable above — reported separately because the two can also come apart.

Suspicion — not established

A 5 Gbps device enumerated on the USB 2 half heuristic

The SuperSpeed side of this port has a companion port that is populated while the SuperSpeed half sits idle. That pattern usually means the cable has no SuperSpeed pairs — but a device that simply chose USB 2 looks identical from here.

→ Swap the cable, keep the device, compare

What cannot be answered here

Whether the drive itself is slow is not decidable without reading from it. The link rate above is what was negotiated, not what the device achieves — a 5 Gbps link and a cheap flash chip look the same until something measures the transfer.
